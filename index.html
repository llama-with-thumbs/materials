Interesting Materials.

A field guide to substances that exist because of one specific trick — a polyurethane that carries tons without overheating, a ceramic that clamps its own cracks shut, a metal with no grains at all. Each entry says what it does that nothing else does, and where you actually meet it.

Photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and remain under their own licences — each card links to its source and licence, and the full list lives in credits.js. Fourteen materials had no freely licensed photograph available; those cards carry a drawn illustration instead, labelled as such, which approximates the real appearance rather than depicting a specific sample. Figures are typical published values for the common grade, not a substitute for a datasheet.
