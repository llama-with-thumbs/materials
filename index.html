interesting materials.

a field guide to 539 materials. the bauhaus sent every student to the workshop before the drawing board: a term of handling metal, wood, paper and cloth, to learn what each will do and what it refuses to do. this catalogue carries that preliminary course on. a polyurethane that carries tons without overheating, a ceramic that clamps its own cracks shut, a lashing that tightens as it dries, a film base that eats itself. each card says why the material is here, and where you actually meet it.

every photograph keeps its own licence and says where it came from — the photographer's own page, an agency, a museum, a maker, or wikimedia commons where nothing else would do — and the full list lives in credits.js. where no photograph of the material itself exists, the card shows the next best thing and says so in red on the picture: something made from it, how it is made, a close relative, or a similar example with a caption saying what it actually is. figures are typical published values for the common grade, not a substitute for a datasheet.

set in jost, after futura, in the manner of the staatliches bauhaus: one typeface, one working colour, everything in lowercase, nothing on the page that is not doing a job.
