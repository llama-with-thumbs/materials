Interesting Materials.

A field guide to 501 substances that exist because of one specific trick — a polyurethane that carries tons without overheating, a ceramic that clamps its own cracks shut, a lashing that tightens itself as it dries, a bag woven closed so it has no seam to burst. Each entry says what it does that nothing else does, and where you actually meet it.

Images come from Wikimedia Commons and remain under their own licences — each card links to its source and licence, and the full list lives in credits.js. Where no photograph of a material exists, the card shows the next best thing and says so in the corner of the image: something made from it, how it is made, a close relative, or — where even that is missing — a similar example, the nearest comparable object, with a caption saying what it actually is. Figures are typical published values for the common grade, not a substitute for a datasheet.
