Interesting Materials.

A field guide to 368 substances that exist because of one specific trick — a polyurethane that carries tons without overheating, a ceramic that clamps its own cracks shut, a lashing that tightens itself as it dries, a bag woven closed so it has no seam to burst. Each entry says what it does that nothing else does, and where you actually meet it.

Images come from Wikimedia Commons and remain under their own licences — each card links to its source and licence, and the full list lives in credits.js. Where no photograph of a material exists, the card shows the next best thing and says so in the corner of the image: its molecular structure, something made from it, how it is made, or a close relative. Fifty-five materials have none of those either; those carry a drawn illustration, labelled as such, which approximates the appearance rather than depicting a real sample. Figures are typical published values for the common grade, not a substitute for a datasheet.
